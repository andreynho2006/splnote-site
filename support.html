SPLNOTE Support

Email the developer through Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight. Tell us your iOS version and what you tapped before the problem showed up.

A friend can't open my invite

Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive turned on (Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud). Then send the invite link again from the group.

Changes don't show up on another phone

Sync runs through iCloud and can take a minute. Check that both phones are online and signed in to iCloud, then reopen the group.

I can't edit an expense

Only the person who created the group can edit expenses and invite people. Members can add expenses and payments; only the admin can change existing expenses.

I bought Premium on another device

Open Settings in SPLNOTE and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How many groups are free?

You can create 3 groups for free. Groups you join from an invite don't count. Premium is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited groups.
