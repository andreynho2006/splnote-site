Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to create an account?

No SPLNOTE account or signup is needed. To share a group, you and your friends need SPLNOTE on iPhone and iCloud enabled.

Can I use it without sharing a group?

Yes. You can add everyone’s expenses and keep the group’s balance on your own iPhone.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Add expenses without a connection. Shared groups sync through iCloud when you’re back online.

Is $4.99 really a one-time payment?

Yes. The first 3 groups you create are free. Premium unlocks unlimited groups with a single $4.99 purchase, with no subscription.

Can I use different currencies in a group?

Each group uses one currency. SPLNOTE does not convert currencies.

Does SPLNOTE send payments?

No. SPLNOTE suggests who should pay whom and lets you record payments. Send the money however you normally pay each other.
