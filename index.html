good to know

Do I need an account?

No. There is no SPLNOTE account and no signup. Open the app and start a group.

Can one person keep the whole tab?

Yes. Add everyone's expenses on your own iPhone and keep the group's balance there.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes. Your groups live on your iPhone, so you can add an expense on a plane or in a basement bar.

Is $4.99 really a one-time payment?

Yes. The first 3 groups you create are free. A single $4.99 purchase unlocks unlimited groups, with no subscription.

What does SETTLED mean?

The stamp appears when every balance in the group is exactly zero. Until then, the tab shows who still owes whom.

Does SPLNOTE send money?

No. It works out who should pay whom and lets you record payments. Send the money however you normally pay each other.

Can a group mix currencies?

Each group uses one currency. SPLNOTE doesn't convert between currencies.

Where can I get help?

Visit SPLNOTE Support for help with groups and purchases.
